Privacy Policy - Easy Invoice Generator

Effective date: 11/11/2026

Developer: KESODEV Digital Solutions

Contact: kesodevinfodesk@gmail.com

Easy Invoice Generator ("the app") helps you create invoices, estimates, pro forma invoices and delivery notes on your Android device. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter: business details (name, address, tax/business IDs, phone, email, website, bank/M-Pesa payment details), logo and signature images, clients, items, expenses, invoices, payments, receipts, delivery notes, stamp image and settings. This data is stored only on your device. We do not run a server, there is no account or sign-in, and we do not receive, view or store this data.

2. Data you choose to send out

  • PDF / email: when you tap "Send by email" or "Preview PDF", the app creates a PDF and hands it to the email or PDF app you pick. What happens next is governed by that app's provider.
  • Print: when you tap Print, the PDF is passed to Android's system print service (which may send it to a printer or save it as PDF).
  • Backup by email: when you tap "Backup by email", the app creates a backup file (including your business details, clients, documents, logo and signature) and hands it to your email app. We do not receive it. Anyone with access to that email can read it, so keep it private.

3. Advertising (Free plan)

The Free plan shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and diagnose problems. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Premium subscribers see no ads. You can also choose to watch a rewarded video ad (full-screen) to unlock premium features for 30 days; the same ad provider data described above applies. Premium features unlocked this way expire after 30 days unless renewed.

4. Purchases

The Premium yearly subscription is sold and billed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. The app checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load ads and verify your subscription.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK on the Free plan.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files beyond images you pick yourself.

6. Children

The app is intended for business users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Use Settings > Delete all my data or uninstall the app. Backup files you emailed to yourself must be deleted from your email account. Data handled by Google (ads, Google Play) is governed by Google's policies.

8. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Network traffic by the ads and billing SDKs uses encrypted connections. No method of storage is perfectly secure; protect your device with a screen lock.

9. Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, you control it directly in the app. If you are in Kenya, the EU/UK or elsewhere with data protection law, you may contact us at the address above with any privacy question.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change and the new policy will be published at the same URL.
